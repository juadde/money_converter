Convert any amount between 38 currencies with the official reference rates of the European Central Bank (ECB).

LIVE CONVERSION
The result appears as you type. Pick both currencies from a searchable list, by code or by name, and swap them in one tap.

ECB RATES
The ECB publishes its reference rates every working day around 16:00 CET. The app fetches them straight from the source, with a backup copy if the ECB website does not answer.

HISTORY CHART
See how the selected pair moved over 1 week, 1 month or 3 months. Touch the chart to read the value of a given day.

CURRENCIES PEGGED TO THE EURO
On top of the ECB currencies: Cape Verdean escudo, CFA francs (WAEMU and CEMAC), Comorian franc, CFP franc, Bosnia-Herzegovina convertible mark and São Tomé and Príncipe dobra, computed from their official parity with the euro.

WORKS OFFLINE
The last downloaded rates stay available without a connection.

SIMPLE AND RESPECTFUL
